Considering a money transfer to **Sri Lanka**? This independent guide explains how to check ACE Money Transfer availability, compare costs and review a referral offer before paying.

## Before sending money to Sri Lanka

1. Check whether ACE currently supports transfers **from your sending country to Sri Lanka**; available corridors can change.
2. Compare the live exchange rate and any transfer fee with other services.
3. Check the recipient's available payout options (such as bank deposit, cash pickup or wallet, if offered).
4. Confirm the amount the recipient will receive and the estimated delivery time.
5. Read the current promotional terms before relying on any first-transfer offer.

## First-transfer promotions and referral rewards

ACE may display a standard first-transfer fee waiver for eligible new customers, including customers who do not use a referral code. The €10 / $10 / £10 referral discount is **publisher-confirmed for eligible new customers**; conditions and availability apply. A potential $50 referral reward is intended for qualifying **existing customers who refer friends**, not a guaranteed new-user signup bonus.
