# Contributing

Corrections and updates are welcome, particularly when accompanied by a link to **current official ACE Money Transfer terms**.

Please include:

1. The country or transfer corridor affected.
2. The exact claim being corrected.
3. A dated, publicly accessible source or a description of the offer shown in the official checkout.
4. Whether the offer is limited to new customers, particular currencies, or specific payout methods.

Do not add unverified guarantees, misleading signup bonuses, or duplicate keyword-stuffed pages.
