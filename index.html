FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is 2596599 a guaranteed signup discount?

No. The code is a referral identifier. Check current eligibility and the final quote directly with ACE.

Is the first transfer free?

ACE may waive its standard transfer fee for eligible new customers, with or without a referral code. Exchange-rate margins and other costs can still apply.

Do new customers receive $50?

Invite qualifying friends to earn up to $50 under the referral terms.

Are all listed country routes available?

Not necessarily. Confirm availability for your exact sender and recipient countries on ACE before registering or paying.
