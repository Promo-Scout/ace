## Before you send money

1. Confirm ACE currently supports transfers **from your sending country to the Philippines**.
2. Compare the exchange rate and the final amount the recipient will receive.

4. Review identity verification, payment methods, limits, and estimated delivery times.
5. Check if a first-transfer fee promotion applies to your account.
