# Money Transfer Referral Bonuses: How They Work (2026)

A referral bonus may reward the sender, the invited friend, or both. Conditions can include first-transfer thresholds, eligible countries, time limits and identity verification. A code does not automatically guarantee a bonus.

## Useful checks

- Confirm supported sending and receiving countries.
- Compare the final recipient amount after exchange-rate conversion and fees.
- Check transfer limits, delivery time and refund policies.
- Read the current referral terms before relying on a promotional claim.
